DocuMint

Ask a document a question, get an answer you can trace.

Upload a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel file. It is split into chunks, embedded, and stored in Postgres. Every answer comes back with citations pointing at the chunk it came from, because an answer you cannot check is not worth much.
